Challenges keep separate saves: Lean Start begins with 120 credits, Fragile Core with 5 lives, and No Rails bans Rail towers. Clear wave 5 in a challenge to earn its medal. Each map has one fixed route for every wave. At level 2 choose Rapid for faster shots or Chain for a second nearby target. Clear wave 5 on a sector to unlock the next map. A completed sector remains playable for endless waves. Choose among the maps above; each map has its own saved run. Every fifth wave ends with the Dread Carrier boss. Its health bar appears over the grid, and a breach costs 4 core health. Enemies: yellow runners move fast; orange armored units resist damage and cost 2 core health; violet splitters burst into two pink shards. Tap a built tower to change its target: First aims at the enemy furthest along the route; Strongest aims at the highest maximum health; Nearest aims at the closest to that tower. You can also upgrade or sell it. Wave rewards fund new defenses. Energy refills when enemies fall. The blast damages and slows every enemy; Boost doubles one selected tower’s damage for 9 seconds.
