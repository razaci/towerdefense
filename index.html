How to play · arsenal & controls

Waves launch automatically after a short break; build and upgrade during the attack. Inferno burns enemies over time; Mortar blasts nearby enemies. Gravity pins one target and slows its neighbors; Tesla arcs into a cluster and briefly stuns targets. Siege pierces armor and strikes bosses hard; Nova pulses damage across its full range. Each map has one fixed route for every wave. At level 2 choose Rapid for faster shots or Chain for a second nearby target. Upgrades add visible armor, Rapid turbines, or Chain capacitors. Clear wave 5 on a sector to unlock the next map. A completed sector remains playable for endless waves. Choose among the maps above; each map has its own saved run. Every fifth wave ends with the Dread Carrier boss. Its health bar appears over the grid, and a breach costs 4 core health. Enemies fight back: runners dash, damaged armored units surge, green repair drones heal allies, and bosses summon escorts as they lose health. Violet splitters burst into two pink shards. Tap a built tower to change its target: First aims at the enemy furthest along the route; Strongest aims at the highest maximum health; Nearest aims at the closest to that tower. You can also upgrade or sell it. Wave rewards fund new defenses. Energy refills when enemies fall. The blast damages and slows every enemy; Boost doubles one selected tower’s damage for 9 seconds. Cleared waves restart automatically after a short build break. Progress saves on this device. Keyboard: 1–9 choose a tower; Space starts or pauses.
